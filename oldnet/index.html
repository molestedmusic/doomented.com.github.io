BAND HISTORY

Doomented is a Canadian doom metal band formed in Vernon, British Columbia, by Art Carriere and Chris Lepodvin in 1996. Following Lepodvin’s departure, the lineup expanded to include Ted Corlett on bass and Craig Watts on drums. From 1997 to 2014, the band performed at venues throughout Vernon, Teulon, and Winnipeg, including a notable appearance at The Zoo in 2004, before concluding its live career in 2014. Although much of the band’s material remained confined to live performances and demo recordings, plans are underway to record the band’s catalogue in full across two new studio albums.
